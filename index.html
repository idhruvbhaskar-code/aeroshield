Our Story

Aeroshield is a pioneering sports startup dedicated to building customized, high-performance cricket guards for youth players. We perfectly blend certified impact protection with feather-light flexibility, ensuring kids stay safe without sacrificing speed or agility on the pitch.

Our journey started with Dhruv Bhaskar, a passionate Class 6 student at Christ Academy School in Electronic City. Frustrated by bulky, ill-fitting traditional cricket pads that slowed down his footwork, Bhaskar inspired us to rethink youth cricket protection from scratch. Erosheind was born to help young athletes play completely fearless.
